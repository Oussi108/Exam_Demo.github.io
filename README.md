# GetHSK · HSK 2.0 Level 3 Exam Demo

Interactive practice exam player with **5 original HSK 2.0 Level 3 practice papers** (80 questions each: 40 Listening, 30 Reading, 10 Writing), bundled with full audio recordings, transcripts, pinyin, word dictionary lookup, and answer keys.

---

## Features

- **5 Complete Practice Papers:** Each paper includes 40 listening, 30 reading, and 10 writing items.
- **Timed Mode:** 85-minute countdown timer and single-playback listening audio to simulate real exam conditions.
- **Learning Mode:** Unlimited audio replays, slow playback (`0.75x`), listening transcripts, toggleable pinyin, interactive Chinese word lookup, and instant answer checking.
- **Offline & Zero Dependencies:** Runs entirely in the browser with no server, account, or installation required. Progress is saved locally in your browser (`localStorage`).
- **Built-in Feedback Tool:** Reviewers can write notes and export a `.txt` feedback file directly from the app.

---

## How to Use

### Option 1: Use Online (GitHub Pages)
Open the live site URL directly in any modern browser:
> https://oussi108.github.io/Exam_Demo.github.io/

### Option 2: Use Offline (Download ZIP)
1. Click **Code → Download ZIP** on GitHub.
2. **Extract the entire ZIP** to a normal folder on your computer (do not open `index.html` from inside the ZIP archive, and keep the `assets/` folder next to `index.html`).
3. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.

---

## Giving Feedback

1. Open **Give feedback** at the bottom of the home screen or results screen.
2. Include the **paper number** and **question number** (e.g., *Paper 02, Question 7*), along with notes on unclear instructions, audio quality, answer keys, or layout issues.
3. Click **Download feedback** (or copy the generated text block) and send it back to the person who shared the demo.

---

## Disclaimer

These original GetHSK draft practice papers are provided for review and practice feedback. They are **not** official HSK papers, and scores are practice results rather than official HSK scores.
