GETHSK · HSK 2.0 LEVEL 3 EXAM DEMO

1. Extract the whole ZIP to a normal folder. Do not open index.html from
   inside the ZIP, and keep the assets folder beside it.
2. Open index.html in a recent browser. No sign-in, installation, internet,
   or GetHSK server is needed.
3. Choose one of four GetHSK Real papers. Timed mode has an 85-minute
   countdown and one playback of each listening recording at normal speed.
   Learning and review support 1×, 0.75×, 0.6×, and 0.5× playback.
   Learning mode also offers transcripts, pinyin, and answer checks.
4. Finish for a raw practice score and review. To send feedback, open
   Give feedback, write your notes, and download the text file. Send it back
   to the person who shared the demo. If the download is blocked, copy the
   note that appears below it. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Useful feedback: paper and question number, unclear instructions, audio
quality, whether the keyed answer makes sense, and anything awkward on your
screen. Please leave out passwords and other personal details.

Progress is local to this browser and folder when browser storage is
available. If the page says progress is kept only while the tab is open,
keep that tab open until you finish. The timed countdown does not pause when
you leave the player. Retaking replaces the saved result for that paper and
mode after confirmation.

These reviewer editions use GetHSK Real Papers 01–04, with repeated items
replaced and reading answer banks adapted to the HSK 2.0 Level 3 sample.
The short-dialogue part still uses existing GetHSK recordings that can run
longer than the sample. Timed mode uses a whole-paper countdown and starts
recordings when clicked. The displayed score is a raw practice score.
